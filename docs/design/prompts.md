# Sonora mockup prompts

These exact prompts were submitted to the built-in image-generation tool. No input reference images were used. All boards use an opaque background. The visual directions are proposals, not implemented app screens.

## 01 noche

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity iPhone music-player design exploration board, landscape, with FOUR complete flat front-facing iPhone screen mockups aligned horizontally at equal size. Title on the neutral charcoal board above the phones: "SONORA / 01 NOCHE". Small caption below: "Biblioteca · Álbum · Reproductor · Letras". This is a visual proposal, not an implemented app.
Primary request: Sonora is a serious personal-file music player for iPhone. It imports music from Files and iCloud Drive, supports a rich local library, album covers, song/album/artist metadata and synced lyrics. Create an original, restrained, buildable iOS design with strong typography and artwork, generous spacing, credible controls, native-looking SF-style icons, 44-point touch targets, white text on near-black, a tiny warm amber accent and subtle background color derived from the selected album artwork. Sophisticated dark direction. No giant brand logo inside screens, no promotional slogans, no dashboard cards, no equalizer decoration, no glows, no decorative visualizer. Practical player UI.
Composition: four straight-on modern iPhone screen silhouettes, tall portrait aspect about 393:852, thin black bezels, small Dynamic Island, status time 9:41 and home indicator. No tilted devices, no hands, no desk, no perspective. All four screens fully visible with margins around entire board. Large enough text to read.
Use the same wholly fictional artist "Mara Sol", album "Horas azules", song "A media luz", and coherent original cover art across all four screens. Original cover: moody blue-hour photograph of a rocky coast with a small warm yellow sun near the horizon, subtle grain, no typography on artwork. Other library covers should be original, varied and beautiful photography/abstract art, never real existing covers. Mock library totals are sample data. Spanish UI.
Screen 1 Biblioteca: large header "Biblioteca", import plus button, search field "Buscar en tu música", tabs "Álbumes", "Artistas", "Canciones". Albumes selected. A neat two-column grid of 6 large album artworks with compact album title and artist beneath. Include Horas azules/Mara Sol plus fictional "Mareas"/"Luz Norte", "Después"/"Isla", "Rituales"/"Nexo". At bottom persistent mini-player with small current artwork, "A media luz", "Mara Sol", pause and next buttons, thin progress. Under it native-style tab bar Biblioteca / Playlists / Ajustes, Biblioteca selected.
Screen 2 Álbum: back chevron, overflow, medium-large centered square Horas azules cover, title "Horas azules", artist "Mara Sol" as navigable label, line "2026 · 10 canciones · 42 min", two buttons "Reproducir" and "Aleatorio", a compact track list with track numbers and durations, current song amber. At bottom same mini-player and tab bar. No fake follower counts, no streaming purchase buttons.
Screen 3 Reproductor: down chevron and overflow at top, album title context small, expansive square coastal artwork dominating upper half, "A media luz" prominent, "Mara Sol · Horas azules" secondary, a heart favorite control, modest technical badge "FLAC · 24 bit / 96 kHz" clearly describing file format, timeline labeled "1:42" and "4:28", large previous/pause/next controls, smaller shuffle/repeat controls, a volume slider and output selector "iPhone", bottom icons labeled "Letras" and "Cola". Technical file quality must not claim actual output quality.
Screen 4 Letras: top compact now-playing row with thumbnail and "A media luz" / "Mara Sol", close button, large left-aligned ORIGINAL fictional lyric text with one bright active line and previous/future lines subdued but readable; exact lines "La ciudad se queda en calma" / "y tu voz vuelve a sonar" / "guardo luz en los bolsillos" / "para verte regresar". Active line "y tu voz vuelve a sonar". Small "Letras sincronizadas" label. Bottom thin progress and previous/pause/next. Do not depict real song lyrics. Keep controls accessible and not too dense.
Quality: polished coherent product UI, crisp readable Spanish labels, convincing spacing and hierarchy, album artwork is the hero. No watermark, no implementation code.
```
